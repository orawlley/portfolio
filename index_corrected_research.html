I am a Chanakya Ph.D. Fellow Awardee sponsored by DRISHTI CPS, Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore) . I have completed my Ph.D. in the broad area of Computer Vision in Autonomous Vehicles in the Disruptive Technologies Lab, Department of CSIS, BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus. I was advised by Dr. Shashank Gupta .

My research interests are majorly in Internet of Vehicles (IoV), Edge Computing and Computer Vision in Autonomous Vehicles. I am a student member of IEEE, ACM and an affiliate member of DRISHTI CPS, IIT Indore. I am also a review committee member of many journals, transactions and conferences of good international repute.

I have also received an International Travel Support (ITS) Fellowship, ANRF , India, for presenting a manuscript in the broad domain of Intelligent Transportation Systems at the 35th IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV 2024) , held in Jeju Island, South Korea.

My research particularly piques interest in the AI-driven modelling of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) in the broader domain of Edge Computing (EC) and Computer Vision in Autonomous Vehicles (AV). I am also interested in exploring the interplay between EC and AI to address practical perception challenges in vehicular environments and tackle energy-related concerns within IoV systems. I aim to work towards establishing a conducive ecosystem by enhancing and advancing Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) through vehicular networking technologies.
